The first time you open AntiGem, it walks you through three short screens.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Allow permissions

**Notifications** is required: it keeps the background service running. **Files** and **Microphone** are optional; you can change them later in Android settings.

![First launch](/screens/agent_picker.webp "phone")

### Choose your agents

Tick any mix of Antigravity, Claude Code, OpenCode and Codex. Only the agents you pick run. You can change this later in **Settings → Agents**.

### Set up Linux

Pick **Full environment** (about 350 MB, recommended), **Minimal** (about 33 MB) or **From a ZIP file**. It installs in the background, so you can keep using the app.

### Sign in

Open a new chat, pick your agent, and sign in to its account or add an API key.

:::

## Good to know

- Tap **Continue in background** to use the app while Linux installs.
- Only plan to connect to a PC or server? Tap **Skip** on the Linux screen.
