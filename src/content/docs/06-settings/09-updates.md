**Where:** ☰ → **Settings** → **Updates**, and ☰ → **Settings** → **About & Info** → **What's new**

How updates work (the update dialog, critical updates, updating by hand) is explained on [Updating and uninstalling](../01-getting-started/09-updating-and-uninstalling.md). This page lists the options.

## Beta updates

### Get beta updates

**Default:** off. Early versions with new features before everyone else. They can have bugs.

Turning it on asks **Get beta updates?**: once a beta is installed you can't go back to the stable version until the next stable release (uninstalling would delete your data). Tap **Turn on** to confirm.

The text under the switch says where you are:

- **You get the stable releases only.**
- **You get each new beta as soon as it's out, and the stable releases.**
- On a beta with betas turned off: **No more betas. You stay on this one until the next stable release, which then comes as a normal update.**

## Background checks

### Check in the background

**Default:** on, every 12 hours. Notifies you when a new version is out, even when AntiGem is closed. Set how often: a number from 1 to 999 and a unit. Off: **No background checks. AntiGem still checks every time you open it.**

## What's new

**About & Info → What's new** lists every AntiGem version, newest first, with what changed. Your version is marked **Installed**, the newest **Latest**, and a beta that isn't out yet **Not released yet**. Tap a version to **Expand** or **Collapse** its notes. The top line says **You're on vX, the latest** or which version you're on.
