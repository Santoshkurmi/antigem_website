Pick a theme from the gallery, or make your own.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Open the theme gallery

**Settings → Themes**.

![Themes](/screens/themes_light.webp "phone")

### Pick one

Light and dark themes apply right away.

### Make your own

Edit colors, or ask an agent to design one with the Theme MCP.

:::

## Good to know

- Themes can be imported and exported.
