Everything in AntiGem can be set up from the settings screen.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Open settings

From the side bar.

![Settings](/screens/settings.webp "phone")

### Find a setting

Settings are grouped: agents, providers, servers, MCP, Jarvis, appearance, backups and more.

### Back up

**Settings → Backups & Restore** saves the Linux environment, projects and chats.
