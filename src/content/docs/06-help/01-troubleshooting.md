Most problems have a quick fix.

## Step by step

:::steps
### The agent doesn't answer

Tap the stop button, pick another model or refresh the model list.

### The app stops in the background

Set **Settings → Apps → AntiGem → Battery** to **Unrestricted** in Android.

### Jarvis doesn't hear you

Allow the **Microphone** permission, and check **Settings → Jarvis** is on.

:::
