At the end, you can talk to AntiGem: open projects, start coding tasks, answer the agent's questions and approvals, and hear the results, without touching the screen.

:::steps
### Turn on Jarvis

**Settings → Jarvis** → **Turn on Jarvis**. It's off by default.

### Pick the agent

Tap **Voice agent & model** and pick **Claude Code**, **Antigravity**, **OpenCode** or **Codex**, and a **Model** if you like. Until you pick one, Jarvis can't answer. A fast model feels best for voice: Jarvis hands big jobs to separate task chats anyway.

### Finish Setup

Tap **Setup**:

- **Add the Jarvis MCP server** (required): the tools Jarvis controls the app with.
- **Let Jarvis' tools run without asking** (Claude Code only), or every spoken answer would need an approval.
- **Allow the microphone.**
- **Install the Jarvis skill** (optional).

### Add send phrases

Open **Listening & sending**. Under **Send with a phrase**, tap **Edit**, then **Add suggestions** to add phrases like "send it". The lists start empty.

### Tap the orb and talk

A small orb appears on the edge of the screen. Tap it and say what you want. End with a send phrase, or tap **Send**.
:::

## Things to say

- "Open the todo-app project and show me the main file."
- "Fix the failing tests and tell me when it's done." (a task: it runs in its own chat, and Jarvis tells you when it finishes, fails or needs you)
- "What did the agent change?" / "Read me the last reply."
- "Switch to the dark theme." / "How much of my Claude limit is left?"
- When an approval comes: "allow it" or "no".

## Fully hands-free

Turn on **Hands-free mode** to wake Jarvis with a phrase, even with the screen off. You can also wake it from a Quick Settings tile, the notification or a headset button.

See [Jarvis](../05-power-features/01-jarvis.md) for every setting, the send and cancel phrases, approvals by voice, and troubleshooting.
