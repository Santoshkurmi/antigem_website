MCP servers give agents tools: a browser to drive, a terminal, control of your phone and more. AntiGem ships ten as one-tap presets.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Open Settings → MCP Servers

You'll see the built-in presets.

![MCP servers](/screens/mcp_presets.webp "phone")

### Turn one on

Tap a preset. Every agent can use it from the next message.

### Add your own

Add a local (command) or remote (HTTP) MCP server.

:::

## Good to know

- Built in: Browser, Terminal, Android, Notes & To-dos, Custom Plugin, Plugin Developer, Jarvis, Theme, Stitch and REA.
