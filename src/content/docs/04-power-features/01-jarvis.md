Jarvis is a voice assistant on top of Antigravity, Claude Code, OpenCode or Codex. It is off by default.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Turn it on

Open **Settings → Jarvis** and switch it on.

![Jarvis](/screens/jarvis_settings.webp "phone")

### Wake it

Tap the floating orb, use the Quick Settings tile or the notification, press a headset button, or say the wake phrase.

### Hand over a task

Say something like “fix the failing tests and tell me when it's done”. Jarvis runs it in its own chat and tells you when it finishes, fails or needs you.

:::

## Good to know

- Approvals are read out loud and answered by voice.
- Send with your own phrases (“send it”, “bhej do”), cancel with others.
