AntiGem has a file manager for your projects and phone storage, and notes and to-do lists the agents can read too.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Browse files

Open the Files screen to move, copy, rename and share files.

### Take notes

Keep notes and to-do lists in the app or in the floating bubble.

### Let agents help

Turn on the Notes MCP so agents can read and update them.

:::
