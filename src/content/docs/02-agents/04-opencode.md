OpenCode is the open-source coding agent. It works with 200+ providers with your own keys, any OpenAI-compatible API, and free models.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Open Settings → OpenCode

Here you manage the server, providers, custom endpoints and approvals.

![OpenCode](/screens/model_picker_usage.webp "phone")

### Connect a provider

Sign in with an API key, a browser or device-code sign-in, or credentials already on the machine.

### Add a custom provider

Add any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoint (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, LiteLLM…) and tap **Fetch from endpoint** to load its models.

:::

## Good to know

- OpenCode's own free models work with no account at all.
- Turn models with images, reasoning or tools on and off one by one.
