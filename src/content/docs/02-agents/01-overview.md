AntiGem runs four agents. Each one is its own official CLI, so it behaves just like on a computer. You can turn on several and pick one for each chat.

## Step by step

:::steps
### Open Settings → Agents

Tick the agents you want. The app restarts so that nothing of a turned-off agent keeps running.

![Choosing an agent](/screens/agent_picker.webp "phone")

### Start a chat

On the empty chat screen, pick the agent for the new chat.

### Pick a model

Tap the model name above the message box to see every model of that agent, with your plan's usage.

:::

## Good to know

- **Antigravity**: your Google account, Gemini and Claude models.
- **Claude Code**: a Claude subscription, a Console account, or Anthropic-compatible providers.
- **OpenCode**: 200+ providers, local models and free models.
- **Codex**: your ChatGPT account.
